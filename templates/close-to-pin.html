Analyze swimmer best times against the current time standards to determine qualification status for medal meets.

📋 About Close to Pin Analysis

This report can help coaches determine strategies to help swimmers achieve their goals of qualifying for end of season meets. Using the "Best Times" report from Swimtopia, this tool will give a status for each swimmer on your team based on which meet they currently qualify for. A PDF report is available to send to coaches or swimmers and parents to review.
