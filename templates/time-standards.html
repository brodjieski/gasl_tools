📊 GASL Time Standards Calculator

Calculate new swimming time standards based on historical performance data

🔬 About Time Standards Calculator

Time standards are reviewed every 4 years. This calculator can take result data from 1 or more seasons to determine times that make sense for qualification in end of the season medal meets. The default analysis will determine the top 15% of times for Gold meet qualification and the top 55% of times for Silver meet qualification. The calculator also will attempt to estimate how long the medal meets will last to help with planning. The parameters are adjustable to allow for additional control over the analysis.

Export the Top Times report from the GASL swimtopia site which will be used in this analysis. From the GASL League Swimtopia site, go to Reports->Top Times (New). Select a season, the top 250 times, and the correct event template to generate the report. Then export the .csv file for upload into this tool. Do this for each season to be included in the analysis.
