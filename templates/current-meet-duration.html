📋 About Championship Meet Time Estimator

This tool estimates championship meet duration based on the current GASL time standards. Upload the swimmer data file and configure timing parameters to get estimates for Gold, Silver, and Bronze meet durations along with team attendance summaries.

To get the swimmer data file to upload: From the GASL League Swimtopia site, go to Reports->Top Times (New). Select a season, the top 300 times, and the correct event template to generate the report. Select the export to .csv to save the generated report.

Estimations try to take into account a couple of factors by assuming which meet any individual swimmer will attend for by selecting the highest meet they qualify for. This should be used to get a general idea of meet durations.
