📋 About Current Meet Duration Estimator

This tool estimates meet duration based on the current GASL time standards. Upload swimmer data files and configure timing parameters to get estimates for Gold, Silver, and Bronze meet durations along with team attendance summaries.

Export the Top Times report from the GASL swimtopia site for each season you want to analyze. From the GASL League Swimtopia site, go to Reports->Top Times (New). Select a season, the top 250 times, and the correct event template to generate the report. Then export the .csv file for upload.
