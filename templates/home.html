About GASL Tools

These tools are designed to help the Greater Annapolis Swimming League (GASL) analyze swimming performance data, set appropriate time standards, and support swimmers in understanding their competitive positioning.

  • Time Standards Calculator: Data-driven approach to setting qualification times
  • Close to Pin Analysis: Individual swimmer progress tracking and goal setting
